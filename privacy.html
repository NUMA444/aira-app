AIRA privacy policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

AIRA is a personal assistant used only by its owner. This page explains what it does with Google data.

What AIRA accesses

  • Gmail (read): to summarise mail and spot bills, deadlines and replies the owner is waiting for.
  • Gmail (send): only to send an email after the owner has approved its exact text.
  • Google Calendar events: to read upcoming events and to add, change or remove events the owner asks for.

Where the data goes

Data is processed on the owner's own computer. Sign-in credentials are kept in the macOS Keychain. To answer the owner's questions, relevant text may be sent to the AI model provider he uses (Anthropic). Google data is never sold, never shared with anyone else, never used for advertising and never used to train AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

AIRA's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting data and revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting AIRA deletes its stored credentials and what it derived from the mail.
